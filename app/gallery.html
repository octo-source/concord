01 · Foundations

Paper, ink, one accent

Warm off-white ground, near-black ink, deep teal. Three semantic constants that never change meaning: gold is human, machine blue is machine, signal orange is disagreement. Corrected numbers are solid; uncorrected are hollow or hatched.
